How we deploy

From the first site visit to a system that improves itself.

Our engineers are forward deployed: they sit with the people who run the operation until the system works for them.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We spend time on site with the operator and the people who run the work, and write down what the operation actually needs.

  2. 02

    Design

    A complete system design in days: what to sense, where each model runs, what the object model holds, what it costs and who approves every action.

  3. 03

    Simulate

    The customer watches the operation run on the design before anything is bought or built.

  4. 04

    Deploy

    Our forward deployed engineers stand the platform up inside the customer's perimeter, on their hardware or their cloud.

  5. 05

    Operate

    Every run of the operation trains the customer's own models, so the system gets better the longer it runs.
