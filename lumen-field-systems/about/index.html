About · Austin, Texas

Why we started Lumen Field Systems.

Sovereign AI for the water utilities of Texas

Texas utilities are being asked to do more with fewer certified operators every year. The AI they are offered lives in someone else's cloud and answers to someone else.

We started Lumen so that a utility can own its AI the way it owns its pumps: on its own network, trained on its own operation, approved by its own people.

Who we serve

Mid size municipal water and wastewater utilities in Texas, 50,000 to 500,000 connections, modernising SCADA after the 2021 winter storm.

Why us

We have run the control rooms we now automate. Every system we deploy is designed with the operators who will use it at 3 in the morning, and it runs inside the utility's own walls.

Dana Reyes
Founder and CEO

Fifteen years running SCADA and control rooms for municipal water systems across Central Texas, most recently as operations director for a utility serving 400,000 people.
