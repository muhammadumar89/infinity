Ridgeline Engram

Train sovereign AI models in their weights and in their memory. Ridgeline Engram turns the work an operation does into training signal for the open weight models it hosts. Runs, outcomes and corrections are captured, compressed into versioned principles and applied two ways: into the model's weights, and into a memory the model reads at inference. Training data, weights and memory never leave the perimeter.

What it does

What Ridgeline Engram gives the operation.

Capture

Every run becomes training data: kept as an episode with its outcome, its feedback and the corrections a reviewer made.

Compress

Daily routines distil raw learnings into versioned principles that cite their sources. Contradictions are resolved and what never mattered fades.

Train

Curated episodes and principles become post training data for the open weight models the customer hosts, trained on the customer's own hardware.

Reinflate

The model reads the compressed memory and expands it into the problem in front of it: this site, these regulations, these people.

In depth

Context is the moat

Every operation can host the same open weight models. What compounds is context: what this operation learned, corrected and decided. Ridgeline Engram keeps it, so it survives the next model.

Two training targets, one platform

Stable skills used at volume are trained into the weights, where they are fastest and cheapest to serve. Knowledge that changes every month, regulations, equipment and procedures, is kept in memory, where it is current within hours, readable and reversible. When a stronger base model arrives, the memory carries over on day one and the training run is repeated on it.

Rules every loop keeps

Every claim stands on a source. Every writer has a separate verifier. Every release is reversible: a bad change is reverted by its own commit, a bad principle by its own version. Training data, weights and memory stay inside the perimeter.

Where it runs

Inside the customer's perimeter: on premises, in a private cloud or fully air gapped, on models the customer hosts. Dedicated inference for the customer's sovereign models.

Questions

Questions about Ridgeline Engram.

What is compression and reinflation?

Compression turns many observations into one small principle, resolving contradictions and dropping what never mattered. Reinflation is the model expanding that principle into a new situation.

What goes into the weights and what into memory?

Stable skills used at high volume go into the weights. Knowledge that changes with the operation stays in memory, current within hours and reversible.

What happens when a better base model is released?

It is swapped in. The memory carries over unchanged, and the curated training data is applied to the new base model.
